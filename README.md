# Good Parts

Watch YouTube videos in less time. The best parts play at normal speed, the rest plays faster, and filler and sponsors get skipped.

## Install

1. Download the zip from [Releases](../../releases) and double-click it to unzip.
2. In Chrome, type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** with the switch in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top left) and choose the unzipped `good-parts` folder.

## Use

- Open a YouTube video and press **⏭ Good parts** at the bottom of the player.
- The arrow next to it changes the speed, or picks **Just skip sponsors**.
- Press the gold button again to watch the full video.

## Good to know

- No account or key needed for most videos.
- The video's captions are sent to an AI (Claude) to find the best parts.
- Sponsors are found with [SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app/).
